# 10: Social — publish Templates, fork (career ladder travels)

**What to build:** The library-opening slice: an email-verified learner can publish a Roadmap as a read-only **Template**; other learners browse and Fork it into a fully private working copy — structure, Units, Milestones, ordering, and its Career Ladder mapped to the same Milestones — after which author and forker are completely decoupled. Publish can be deleted/unpublished any time. The safety rails live here: anonymous-only accounts can't publish; a one-click report immediately hides a Template pending review (structural, owner-resolution reuse, no abuse pipeline).

**Blocked by:** 05 · 06 · 07 (renamed model, ladder travel, and imports — the template surface sits on all three).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Publish requires verified email; anonymous-only accounts are structurally refused (D-9), not just UI-hidden.
- [ ] Fork is one click, transactional, and produces a fork independent at the row level afterwards (edit/fork/templates never touch source data).
- [ ] Forked ladder's milestone links re-map to forked milestones; orphan paths are impossible.
- [ ] One-click report hides a template immediately for everyone while the owner retains visibility; delete/unpublish works even mid-fork-wave.
- [ ] Fork surface exercises the merge/copy seams with convex-test; abuse paths (fork storms, self-report) covered.
